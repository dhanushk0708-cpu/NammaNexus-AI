   - Every row in `nammaserve_batch2_providers.csv` explicitly records the exact ratio of accessible reviews to total review count in `review_summary_limitations` (for example, *"Only 6 of 875 total Google Maps reviews were accessible via the Google Places interface"*).
   - Where accessible reviews contained complaints or critical feedback, they were captured faithfully in `review_negative_themes`:
     - **`PROV_B2_007` ([Dust and Pest Solutions](https://www.google.com/maps/place/?q=place_id:ChIJ26WHRtw9rjsRgd8rR0hKG44)):** A 4-star review (`2026-09-28`) noted that while pest control and cleaning quality was good, staff professionalism could be improved.
