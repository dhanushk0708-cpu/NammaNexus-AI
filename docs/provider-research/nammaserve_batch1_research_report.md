## 4. Important Missing Information & Source Limitations

1. **Unpublished Rate Cards Among Independent Local Providers:** Out of 23 unique businesses, only marketplace platforms ([Urban Company](https://www.urbancompany.com/bangalore-plumbers-kengeri-satellite-town), [TechSquadTeam](https://techsquadteam.com/washing-machine-repair-in-kengeri-satellite-town-bangalore)) and larger regional operators ([KMK Home Service](https://kmkhomeservice.com/), [Just Knock Company](https://justknockcompany.com/), and spare-part/kit listings on [RR WATER PURIFIER](http://rrwaterpurifiers.in/)) publish explicit numeric pricing online. Neighbourhood plumbers, electricians, AC technicians, and carpenters in Kengeri and RR Nagar universally quote after a phone discussion or onsite inspection (`PRICE_NOT_PUBLISHED` or `QUOTATION_REQUIRED`).
2. **Marketplace Platform Rating Scope & Technician Anonymity:** Marketplace platforms ([Urban Company](https://www.urbancompany.com/bangalore-ac-service-repair-kengeri) and [TechSquadTeam](https://techsquadteam.com/washing-machine-repair-in-rajarajeshwari-nagar-bangalore)) display aggregate category ratings across Bengaluru or India (e.g., 4.74–4.81 across hundreds of thousands of reviews) rather than Kengeri- or RR Nagar-specific ratings, and assign individual technicians only after booking confirmation. Per project rules, platform-wide ratings were excluded from the `rating` and `review_count` columns and documented in `missing_information` and `notes`.
