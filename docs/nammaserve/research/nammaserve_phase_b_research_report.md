## 1. Scope, method, limitations

- Platforms: Urban Company (UC), NoBroker (NB), Justdial (JD), Sulekha (SK). Categories: AC Repair, Home Cleaning, Plumbing, Electrical Services.
- Method: official platform pages only, fetched on the research date. Prices were taken from visible catalog cards and, where present, rate-card tables on the same page. Search-result snippets were used only to discover pages, not as sole evidence for a verified price (two UC cleaning locality variants appear as notes only).
- Access limits respected: Justdial blocked automated access (bot detection) — no bypass; Sulekha category pages returned ROBOTS_DISALLOWED — only the Sulekha Bangalore city page and homepage were read.
- **Coverage is not exhaustive.** Only the service offerings listed here were examined. Prices are 'as displayed', can change with time, locality and promotions, and several are 'starts at' prices.
- UC pages are locality-specific (AC: Electronic City; plumbing/electrical: Richmond Town address; cleaning: Cooke Town). UC cleaning starting prices varied across other Bengaluru localities in search snippets, so treat UC cleaning numbers as locality samples.
- NB pages are city-level. **NB shows two sets of numbers on the same page** (booking catalog cards vs. a 'rate card/charges' table) that frequently disagree. The catalog card is recorded as the displayed price; rate-table values are kept in `variants` / `other_prices_on_same_page`.
- No ratings, reviews, provider names or availability were collected or used.
