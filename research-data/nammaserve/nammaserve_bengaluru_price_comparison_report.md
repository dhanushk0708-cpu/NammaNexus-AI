## Executive Summary and Platform Pricing Models

Publicly available home service pricing in Bengaluru splits into two distinct structural models across the four evaluated platforms:

1. **Managed Standardized Marketplaces ([Urban Company](https://www.urbancompany.com/bangalore) and [NoBroker](https://www.nobroker.in/home-services)):** Both platforms operate centralized, task-level rate cards with upfront `FIXED` and `STARTING_FROM` prices in INR that apply citywide across Bengaluru. Both charge a nominal on-site visitation or inspection fee of INR 48–49 for trade services ([Urban Company Plumbers](https://www.urbancompany.com/bangalore-plumbers), [Urban Company Electricians](https://www.urbancompany.com/bangalore-electricians), [Urban Company Carpenters](https://www.urbancompany.com/bangalore-carpenters), [NoBroker Plumbing](https://www.nobroker.in/plumbing-services-in-bangalore), [NoBroker Electrician](https://www.nobroker.in/electrician-services-in-bangalore), [NoBroker Carpentry](https://www.nobroker.in/carpentry-services-in-bangalore)) and INR 193–299 for appliance/AC diagnostics ([Urban Company AC Service](https://www.urbancompany.com/bangalore-ac-service), [Urban Company RO Repair](https://www.urbancompany.com/bangalore-ro-repair), [NoBroker AC Repair](https://www.nobroker.in/ac-repair-services-in-bangalore), [NoBroker Appliance Services](https://www.nobroker.in/appliance-services-in-bangalore)), which is adjusted against the final invoice when a repair is availed.
2. **Lead-Generation and Directory Aggregators ([Justdial](https://www.justdial.com/Bangalore/24-Hours-Plumbers/nct-12362748) and [Sulekha](https://www.sulekha.com/)):** Neither platform enforces a binding platform-wide checkout rate card. [Justdial](https://www.justdial.com/Bangalore/24-Hours-Plumbers/nct-12362748) displays vendor-submitted catalog prices (`FIXED`, `STARTING_FROM`, or `PRICE_RANGE`) on select Bengaluru business cards while leaving other vendors as `QUOTATION_REQUIRED` ("Call Now / Send Enquiry") or `UNPUBLISHED`. [Sulekha](https://www.sulekha.com/pest-control-services/bangalore) routes all individual vendor bookings through a lead-capture form (`QUOTATION_REQUIRED`), but publishes platform-level approximate cost guide tables at the bottom of five category pages ([AC Service](https://www.sulekha.com/ac-repair-services/jp-nagar-bangalore), [Electrical](https://www.sulekha.com/electricians/bangalore), [Sofa Cleaning](https://www.sulekha.com/sofa-cleaning-services/bangalore), [Pest Control](https://www.sulekha.com/pest-control-services/bangalore), [Refrigerator Repair](https://www.sulekha.com/refrigerator-repair-services/bangalore), and [Water Purifier Service](https://www.sulekha.com/water-purifier-services/bangalore)), while leaving [Plumbing](https://www.sulekha.com/plumbers/basavanagudi-bangalore) and [Carpentry](https://www.sulekha.com/carpenters/jayanagar-bangalore) without standard rate tables.

## Geographic Applicability and Data Limitations

1. **Citywide vs. Locality-Specific Applicability:** [Urban Company](https://www.urbancompany.com/bangalore) and [NoBroker](https://www.nobroker.in/plumbing-services-in-bangalore) explicitly confirm citywide applicability across Bengaluru for their standard rate cards. Conversely, [Justdial](https://www.justdial.com/Bangalore/24-Hours-Plumbers/nct-12362748) prices are tied to individual local vendors in specific neighbourhoods (such as Uttarahalli, BTM Layout, HSR Layout, Gottigere, Halasuru, or Yelahanka); whether those vendors honour the same visit fee across all of Bengaluru is unconfirmed and marked accordingly in the CSV.
2. **Cross-City Directory Contamination:** On [Justdial's Malleswaram AC Cleaning page](https://www.justdial.com/Bangalore/AC-Cleaning-Services-in-Malleswaram/nct-11994579), two sponsored/listed providers (`Chennai Cooltech Enterprises` and `1 Touch Deep Cleaning`) display Tiruvannamalai addresses despite appearing in the Bengaluru directory. Similarly, [Sulekha's Bangalore Electricians page](https://www.sulekha.com/electricians/bangalore) contains a reference to Mumbai in FAQ #9, and [Sulekha's Bangalore Pest Control table](https://www.sulekha.com/pest-control-services/bangalore) is identical to its Tumkur and Kolar tables. All such records are flagged as `Unconfirmed` or noted in `nammaserve_bengaluru_price_comparison.csv`.
3. **Dual Pricing Layers on NoBroker Pages:** [NoBroker's Bengaluru pages](https://www.nobroker.in/plumbing-services-in-bangalore) frequently display two slightly different price sets on the same URL: the live interactive booking cards at the top of the page (e.g., Tap Repair at INR 77, Foam Blast AC Service starting at INR 629, Washing Machine Check-up at INR 199, Occupied Kitchen Power Steam Cleaning at INR 1,959) and the static SEO rate tables further down the page (e.g., Tap Repair at INR 52 Basic / INR 44 VIP, Foam Blast AC Service at INR 599, Washing Machine Check-up at INR 99, Occupied Kitchen Power Steam Cleaning at INR 1,439). Both figures are preserved and cross-referenced in the CSV notes so NammaServe can benchmark against both the live checkout price and the published base/VIP tables.
4. **Exclusion of Spare Parts, Materials, and Taxes:** Across all four platforms, published labour/service prices for Plumbing, Electrical, Carpentry, AC Repair, and Appliance Repair exclude physical materials, spare parts, copper piping, masonry, and ladders unless explicitly stated (such as complete RO filter kits).
